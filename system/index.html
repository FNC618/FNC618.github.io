BLACK WIDOW is a modular research platform composed of interconnected technologies working together.

Autonomous systems designed for specific mission profiles.

Mission OS

Software orchestration layer for planning, simulation, and evidence workflows.

Digital Twin

High-fidelity virtual replica for simulation and validation.

Simulation

Computational models for testing scenarios before physical deployment.

Physical Validation

Real-world experiments and measurements to verify simulation results.

Evidence

Documented proof of technology readiness from simulation and experiments.
